Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

VDT Apps respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use VDT Apps websites, mobile applications, and related services.

Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the particular app or service you use. An app may process information that you provide directly, information required to provide its features, and limited technical information needed for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

VDT Apps does not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific feature and clearly explained to the user.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide and improve app features, maintain security, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, diagnose technical problems, and operate the service.

Third-Party Services

Some apps may use third-party services such as hosting, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, advertising, or payment services. Any third-party service actually used by a particular app will be governed by its applicable terms and privacy practices.

Payments

If a VDT Apps service offers paid features, payments may be processed through a third-party payment provider such as Razorpay. VDT Apps does not receive or store your complete card, UPI, or banking credentials through the website. Payment information is handled by the applicable payment provider according to its policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by VDT Apps. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Some VDT Apps products may be designed for educational use by children. Where applicable, the specific app's privacy disclosures and applicable child-safety requirements will apply. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions or concerns.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant service, legal requirements, security, or legitimate operational purposes. If an app provides an account or stores user information, the app's specific instructions will explain available deletion options.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services or practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
